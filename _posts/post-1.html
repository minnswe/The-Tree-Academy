What Is Artificial Intelligence?

An introduction to artificial intelligence and its role in modern life.

Artificial intelligence (AI) is a field of technology concerned with creating systems that can perform tasks that normally require aspects of human intelligence.

Learning

Some AI systems can learn patterns from data. Machine learning is one important approach used to develop such systems.

Reasoning

AI systems can also be designed to use information and rules to draw conclusions, solve problems, or support decisions.

AI in Education

AI is increasingly being used in education for applications such as personalized learning, feedback, content creation, and educational analytics.

As AI becomes more widely available, understanding its capabilities and limitations is becoming an important digital skill.
